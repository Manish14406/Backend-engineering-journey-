# Car Wash Booking Engine

A production-oriented backend system for managing
time-based car wash bookings and preventing booking conflicts.

## Engineering Problem

A wash center has:
- limited wash bays
- different service durations
- operating hours
- multiple customers

The backend must ensure that bookings follow
business rules and do not conflict.

## What I Learned

### 1. Domain Modeling
Designed relationships between users, plans,
wash bays, operating hours and bookings.

### 2. Business Logic
Implemented booking validation, availability
checks and conflict detection.

### 3. Data Integrity
Used relational constraints, migrations and
database-level design to maintain consistent data.

### 4. Concurrency
Analyzed the race condition where two requests
attempt to reserve the same bay simultaneously.

### 5. Testing
Built tests around booking rules, conflicts,
validation and authorization.
